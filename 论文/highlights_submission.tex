{\large\bfseries Highlights}\\[2pt]
{\itshape Field accuracy is not heat accuracy: wall-flux-constrained}\\[2pt]
{\itshape surrogates for non-Boussinesq convection}
\end{center}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{8pt}
\item Field error below 1\% can coexist with $>2\%$ $Nu$ error in 43\% of cavity samples.
\item Wall-flux loss reduces matched six-point error against a non-converged $64^2$ target.
\item In a two-seed 4-by-4 matrix, every trained stencil is optimal only at its own order.
\item A 134-family bootstrap leaves magnitude and temperature-curvature sign unresolved.
\item Contrast scaling and 3D tests are consistency checks, not general validation.
\end{itemize}
