{\large\bfseries Highlights}\\[2pt]
{\itshape Field accuracy is not heat accuracy: wall-flux-constrained}\\[2pt]
{\itshape surrogates for non-Boussinesq convection}
\end{center}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{8pt}
\item Field error below 1\% leaves $>2\%$ $Nu$ error in 66\% of 200 held-out samples.
\item A wall-flux penalty cuts integral $Nu$ error from 4.0\% to 0.6\%.
\item Boussinesq--compressible gap factorises as $D=\varepsilon P(X)$ over 627 pairs.
\item Single-contrast data are reusable within one boundary-condition family.
\item Scaling law is the reusable object; rescaled plain matches within a few per cent.
\end{itemize}
