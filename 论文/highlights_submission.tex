\begin{center}
{\large\bfseries Highlights}\\[2pt]
{\itshape Field accuracy is not heat accuracy: wall-flux-constrained}\\[2pt]
{\itshape surrogates for non-Boussinesq convection}
\end{center}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{8pt}
\item On 200 held-out classical-cavity samples, 0.09\% field error coexists with 2.0\% integral $Nu$ error.
\item A 22-sample random-wall replication gives 0.47\% field error and 4.04\% $Nu$ error.
\item A wall-flux penalty cuts both-wall $Nu$ error to 0.01\% (classical) and 0.58\% (random walls).
\item Boussinesq--compressible gap factorises as $D=\varepsilon P(X)$ over 627 pairs.
\item External $\varepsilon$ factorisation is reusable; rescaled plain matches within 0.2\%.
\end{itemize}
