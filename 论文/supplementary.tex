\title{Supplementary material for:\\
The Boussinesq--compressible gap in steady two-dimensional buoyancy-driven enclosure convection}
\author[a]{Author(s)}
\address[a]{Affiliation(s)}
\begin{abstract}
This supplement collects the tables that support the main text but are not needed in the flow of
the argument: the asymptotic predictions and their numerical confirmation, the two-parameter fits,
the exact single-$\varepsilon$ transfer matrix, the learning curve, the public-data splits, the
prefactor--velocity law, and the mapping from scripts to reported results. Table and section
numbers in cross-references refer to the main text.
\end{abstract}
\end{frontmatter}

The runs behind every number in the main text are: 600 main solutions
(\path{run_batch.sh}), 100 paired (\path{run_paired.sh}), 27 at
$\varepsilon\to0$ (\path{run_eps0.sh}), 104 at $Pr=4.38$ (\path{run_water.sh});
\path{run_cpu_queue.sh} adds the 2:1 geometry and the tight-tolerance reruns.
